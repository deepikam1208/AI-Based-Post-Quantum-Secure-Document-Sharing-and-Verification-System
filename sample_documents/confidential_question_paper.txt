SECTION A (Compulsory - Answer all questions, 5 x 4 = 20 Marks)
1. Explain Shor's algorithm and contrast its polynomial-time impact on RSA/ECC versus symmetric ciphers.
2. Outline the mathematical foundation of Learning With Errors (LWE) and Ring-LWE problems.
3. Why does Grover's algorithm only achieve quadratic speedup against AES-256 and SHA-256?
4. Differentiate between Key Encapsulation Mechanisms (KEM) and traditional Public Key Encryption (PKE).
5. State the security levels defined by NIST for Post-Quantum Cryptography standards (Levels 1 through 5).

SECTION B (Answer any 4 out of 6 questions, 4 x 20 = 80 Marks)
6. (a) Provide the complete mathematical specification for NIST FIPS 203 (ML-KEM-768).
   (b) Detail the public key compression, matrix-vector multiplication over R_q, and Fujisaki-Okamoto transform. (10 + 10 Marks)

7. (a) Compare the signature verification mechanism of NIST FIPS 204 (ML-DSA-65) with classic Ed25519.
   (b) Explain rejection sampling in lattice digital signatures and how side-channel attacks are mitigated. (10 + 10 Marks)

8. (a) Design an end-to-end hybrid cryptosystem that integrates X25519 with Kyber-768 for TLS 1.3 handshake.
   (b) How does authenticated encryption using AES-256-GCM guarantee non-repudiation when coupled with ML-DSA? (12 + 8 Marks)

9. (a) Detail the stateful hash-based signature scheme XMSS (RFC 8391) versus stateless SLH-DSA (FIPS 205).
   (b) Discuss the operational hazards of OTS (One-Time Signature) key reuse in embedded systems. (10 + 10 Marks)
